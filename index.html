ABOUT THE DATA

How to read this table

Median OT is the midpoint among employees at each facility who received overtime in 2025. Middle 50% shows the 25th through 75th percentile of overtime payments. Full range shows the lowest and highest overtime payments.

% $100K+ is the percentage of overtime-earning employees at that facility who received at least $100,000 in overtime. It is not the percentage of the facility's entire workforce.

Facilities listed as “Not Provided” are hidden by default.
